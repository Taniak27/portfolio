about me

Hi, I’m Tanya Pishtihan, a front end developer and UI designer based in Spain. I build modern, responsive websites with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Figma, and I also create WordPress Elementor sites that are easy to manage. My focus is clean design, accessibility, and performance so the final result looks great and loads fast.

I enjoy turning complex ideas into simple, user friendly interfaces. Whether it’s a landing page, portfolio, or small business website, I care about structure, typography, and details that improve usability. If you need a reliable developer who can design and build, I’m happy to help.
